# Product Hunt Launch

Use this workflow for a product that is ready for public launch and has visual assets.

## Skill Sequence

1. `skills/planner`
2. `skills/adapter-launch`
3. `skills/reviewer`
4. Optional supporting drafts:
   - `skills/adapter-community-discussion` (for Hacker News, Reddit, etc.)
   - X thread drafted from `knowledge/platforms/x.md`

## Required Assets

- Product URL
- Logo
- Screenshots
- Short demo video or GIF
- Pricing or free-tier explanation
- Differentiators
