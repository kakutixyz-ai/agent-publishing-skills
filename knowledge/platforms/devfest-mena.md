## Language

The primary language for all formal content on DevFest MENA is **English (EN)**. All navigation, session titles, speaker bios, and registration forms are presented in English. This is the *de facto* lingua franca for international tech conferences and the Google Developer community. While the platform serves the Arabic-speaking MENA region, Arabic is used only for informal social media engagement, local logistics, and cultural references in affiliated meetup groups. To maximize reach with the CFP review committee and global audience, publish all formal content—talk proposals, speaker bios, and promotional materials—in English.

## Good Fit

- Projects built on Google technologies (Google Cloud, Firebase, Flutter, Android, Google AI, Gemini, TensorFlow, Go, Angular).
- Open-source tools or libraries that solve a specific technical problem for developers in the MENA region.
- Applications demonstrating scalability, cloud-native architecture, or AI/ML integration.
- Developer tools that improve productivity, testing, or deployment workflows.
- Projects with a clear, demonstrable impact on real-world use cases (e.g., fintech, health tech, edtech, smart cities).

## Poor Fit

- Projects unrelated to software development or technology.
- Projects that are purely marketing campaigns, brand activations, or non-technical product launches.
- Projects that violate Google's content policies or promote unethical practices.
- Projects that are still in concept stage with no working prototype or demo.
- Projects that require proprietary, non-Google, or closed-source vendor lock-in without clear developer benefit.

## Format And Style Notes

- Title is benefit-driven and technical. The title must clearly state what the audience will learn or gain.
- Abstract follows a strict problem-solution-takeaway structure. No fluff, no storytelling.
- Key takeaways are bullet points (3–5 items). Reviewers scan these first.
- Tags are specific Google technologies. Use official product names.
- Tone is professional but excited. Short, punchy sentences. Avoid jargon that is not Google-specific.
- Abstract length is under 300 words. Reviewers read dozens of proposals.
- Use bold for key technologies. This helps reviewers identify relevance immediately.
- Visuals in promotional materials use the official DevFest MENA branding. Dark mode with neon gradients, no generic stock photos.

## Tone

Professional, high-energy, and Google-centric; every word must demonstrate technical depth and regional relevance.
