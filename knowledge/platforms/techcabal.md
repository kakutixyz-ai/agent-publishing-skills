## Trigger Context

Triggered by mentions of 'TechCabal', 'African tech ecosystem', 'African startup news', 'African tech journalism'

## Language

The primary language is **English (Nigerian/British variant)**. All content must use British English spelling conventions (e.g., *colour*, *organise*, *behaviour*, *centre*). Avoid American spellings (e.g., *color*, *organize*). The tone is formal but not academic — direct, declarative sentences with active voice. Industry jargon (e.g., "runway," "unit economics," "GP/LP") is expected without explanation. Non-English content is not accepted. If your project documentation or README is in another language, you must translate it to British English before submitting to TechCabal.

## Good Fit

- African-founded or Africa-focused startups, especially in fintech, logistics, healthtech, and edtech.
- Companies that have raised funding rounds (pre-seed, seed, Series A, etc.) with specific metrics to share.
- Tools or platforms solving uniquely African problems (e.g., mobile money infrastructure, cross-border payment solutions, off-grid energy tech).
- Projects with a clear business model and traction data (e.g., user numbers, revenue growth, market expansion).
- Open-source libraries or frameworks that are being adopted by African developers or companies.

## Poor Fit

- Non-African projects with no connection to the continent (e.g., a US-only restaurant app).
- Consumer social media apps or entertainment products without a clear business angle.
- Projects still in ideation phase with no users, revenue, or traction.
- Purely technical tutorials or developer documentation (e.g., "How to use React hooks").
- Projects that are illegal, unethical, or violate African regulatory frameworks (e.g., cryptocurrency projects without proper licensing).

## Format And Style Notes

- Headlines must be concise and factual.
- Use British English spelling throughout.
- Keep paragraphs short — maximum 3-4 sentences.
- Use active voice and direct statements.
- Include specific data and metrics.
- Attribute quotes clearly with blockquote formatting.
- Use descriptive anchor text for links, not "click here."
- Avoid first-person opinion in standard news pieces.

## Tone

The insider's briefing for the African tech ecosystem — data-driven, authoritative, and serious about the business of building in Africa.
