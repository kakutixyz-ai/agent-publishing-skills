## Language

- **Primary language:** German (Hochdeutsch). Over 95% of active content is in German.
- **Multilingual strategy:** English content is only accepted in specific international tech groups or when targeting expat communities. Using English will significantly reduce organic reach.
- **Critical nuance:** Always use formal *Sie* form unless you are 100% certain the user has agreed to *du*. Avoid English filler phrases like "Let's connect!" or "Excited to share." Use German equivalents like "Ich lade Sie herzlich ein..." or "Ich freue mich auf den Austausch zum Thema..."

## Format And Style Notes

- Use formal, precise language with *Sie* form. Avoid casual or American-style hype.
- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences max) and declarative. Use bold for key data points.
- Use bullet points for lists of requirements, tasks, or features. Use the platform's native bullet list tool.
- Keep posts under 300 words. For longer content, attach a PDF or link to a blog.
- Use a maximum of 3-5 hashtags. Use broad, single-word tags in German.
- Always include a single professional image. Use a headshot or clean infographic. Avoid memes or stock photos of people shaking hands.
- Avoid Markdown formatting. Xing's native editor uses WYSIWYG (rich text). Use the toolbar for bold, italic, and bullet points.
- Use industry-specific German terminology. Show *Fachkompetenz* through precise vocabulary.
