## Trigger Context

Triggered by mentions of 'Misk Hub', 'Misk Foundation', 'Saudi talent platform', or 'Saudi startup community'

## Language

The platform's primary communication language is **Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic)** . All navigation, official announcements, and event listings are in Arabic. English is accepted for technical content (coding tutorials, AI frameworks, startup pitches) but is perceived as less authoritative. **Bilingual content (Arabic body with English technical terms) achieves the highest engagement.** Code snippets and technical jargon should remain in English; all explanatory text must be in Arabic.

## Good Fit

- Projects aligned with Saudi Vision 2030 goals (digital transformation, renewable energy, smart cities, edtech, healthtech)
- Open-source tools or frameworks that enable rapid skill-building (learning platforms, coding bootcamp tools, mentorship matching systems)
- Startup MVPs or prototypes seeking early validation from a curated, ambitious audience
- Arabic-language software projects, localization tools, or Arabic NLP libraries
- Projects with clear social impact metrics targeting Saudi youth employment or entrepreneurship

## Poor Fit

- Mature, enterprise-grade products with no connection to skill development or talent incubation
- Gaming or entertainment projects unrelated to education or professional growth
- Projects built primarily for non-Arabic-speaking markets with no localization potential
- Controversial or politically charged tools (surveillance software, censorship bypass tools, political activism platforms)
- Hobby-level projects with no documentation, no clear roadmap, and no demonstration of competence

## Format And Style Notes

- Lead with opportunity, not problem. Open with what the reader can achieve, not what is broken.
- Use formal but accessible Arabic. Avoid dialect, slang, or overly academic phrasing.
- Keep paragraphs under 3 lines. Users scan for CTAs, not long narratives.
- Include a high-resolution hero image (16:9) relevant to the topic. Text-only posts underperform.
- End every post with a clear, action-oriented CTA. Use "سجل الآن" or "قدم طلبك" with a direct link.
- Avoid emojis except for standard bullet markers (✅, 📅). Overuse looks unprofessional.
- Use H2 and H3 headings only. The platform reserves H1 for page titles.
- Bilingual structure: Arabic body + English technical terms. Code, APIs, and tool names stay in English.

## Tone

A formal, aspirational, and opportunity-driven tone that positions every project as a stepping stone for Saudi talent within the Vision 2030 framework.
