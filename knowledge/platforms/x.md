## Language

The primary communication language on X for global tech, AI, and developer audiences is **English** (American English). Posts in other languages have significantly lower organic reach outside their linguistic region. If your project targets a non-English audience, consider posting in that language but add an English summary thread or use a translation service. The platform's algorithm favors English content for broad distribution. Casual, direct, and slightly informal tone is expected.

## Good Fit

- Projects that produce frequent, incremental updates (daily commits, weekly releases, live metrics). X rewards momentum.
- Developer tools, libraries, and frameworks (e.g., CLI tools, React components, API wrappers) that can be shown with code snippets or GIFs.
- AI/ML models, datasets, or demos where you can share benchmark results, sample outputs, or live links.
- "Build in Public" projects where you are willing to share both wins and failures transparently (e.g., revenue, user growth, technical challenges).
- Projects with strong visual or interactive demos — screenshots, short video clips, or interactive prototypes that fit in a 60-second scroll.

## Poor Fit

- Projects that require lengthy, formal documentation or academic papers to explain (X has a 280-character limit per tweet).
- Enterprise B2B software targeting C-level executives who prefer LinkedIn-style professionalism.
- Hardware projects without a software component or demoable interface (e.g., a new type of screwdriver).
- Projects with no public-facing aspect (e.g., internal tools, proprietary codebases you cannot discuss).
- Mature, slow-moving projects with no new updates or community engagement potential (e.g., a 10-year-old library with no recent commits).

## Format And Style Notes

- Lead with a hook. The first tweet must grab attention in under 2 seconds. Use a question, a surprising statistic, or a bold claim.
- Use threads for depth. Never write a single tweet longer than 250 characters. Use a thread (numbered tweets) for tutorials, case studies, or multi-part insights.
- Show, don't tell. Include a screenshot, GIF, or short video in every post. Visuals increase engagement by 30-40%.
- Be opinionated. Take a stance. Neutral or overly cautious posts get ignored.
- Use plain text formatting. X does not support Markdown. Use line breaks, emojis sparingly, and plain text for structure.
- End with a CTA. Every thread or standalone post should have a clear call to action: follow, visit a link, reply with feedback.
- Tag relevant accounts and trends. Mention key people or projects to increase discoverability, but don't spam.
- Embrace real-time. Post when your audience is active (8-10 AM EST, 7-9 PM EST for US tech). Respond to comments quickly. X rewards recency.
