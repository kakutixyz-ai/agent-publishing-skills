## Trigger Context

Triggered by mentions of 'EU Startups', European tech media, or startup ecosystem outreach.

## Language

- **Primary language:** English (European Business English). Use British/European spelling conventions (e.g., "organisation," "capitalise," "centre").
- **Multilingual strategy:** Articles are published exclusively in English. Non-English content is not accepted. Avoid American idioms and slang. Use neutral, formal vocabulary suitable for a pan-European professional audience.

## Good Fit

- B2B SaaS products or platforms with a clear European market focus or origin
- Startups with recent funding rounds (pre-seed to Series B) that can be reported as news
- Deep tech, fintech, regtech, climate tech, or health tech projects with regulatory awareness
- Products with measurable traction metrics (e.g., user growth, revenue, partnerships) that can be cited
- Founder stories or thought leadership pieces that highlight European innovation and ecosystem growth

## Poor Fit

- Consumer apps or games without a clear business or funding angle
- Projects targeting only non-European markets (e.g., Asia, North America) with no European connection
- Hobby projects, open-source libraries, or developer tools without commercial backing
- Content that is overly technical, academic, or lacking a narrative hook for a business audience
- Controversial topics, political activism, or content that does not align with a professional, optimistic tone

## Format And Style Notes

- Headlines must be clear, benefit-driven, and under 80 characters.
- Lead paragraph must answer who, what, where, why, and how immediately.
- Use very short paragraphs (2-4 sentences max) with generous white space.
- Bullet points are mandatory for listing features, benefits, or key metrics.
- Hyperlinks must use descriptive anchor text, never "click here."
- Include a direct quote from the founder or CEO in every article.
- Use British/European spelling and vocabulary consistently.
- End with a forward-looking statement about future plans or impact.

## Tone

Write like a professional European business journalist: optimistic, data-driven, and ecosystem-focused, with every sentence serving the story of growth and innovation.
