# Anti-Spam Rules

Publishing drafts must prioritize community value over promotion.

Avoid:

- Reposting the same copy across platforms.
- Asking directly for upvotes, likes, stars, retweets, or follows.
- Hype claims such as "revolutionary", "game-changing", or "the best".
- Posting without a working demo, repository, article, or clear evidence.
- Ignoring platform-specific language and formatting norms.

Prefer:

- Specific technical details.
- Clear limitations.
- Reproducible examples.
- Screenshots, demos, or primary links.
- A reason the target community should care.
